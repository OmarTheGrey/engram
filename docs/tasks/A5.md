# A5 Conformance: validity, history, graph and revocation cases

Status: open.

From `docs/tasks/absorb-plan.md`, pull request 4 of 30 (research plans R-010, R-004, R-018 in `docs/research/absorb/long-list.md`). Lane: research. Week: W8. Effort: S. Read AGENTS.md, the plan's entry for this card and `docs/conventions/absorption.md` before starting.

Goal: An adapter that claims `native_validity`, `native_history` or `native_graph` has to show it in the conformance suite, and on every store a revoked item is never admitted by a read through the plane and never outranks its replacement.

Why: ADR 0013's capability flags are claims nothing tests. And arXiv 2609.08258 reports two popular stores returning a revoked policy above its replacement in all 81 of its cells: the plane should make that impossible whatever the store does, and a test should say so.

Steps:

1. A test double with validity, in the test tree: it wraps the fake adapter and truthfully claims `native_validity`, so the suite has a store that claims the capability and one that does not. `packages/adapters/src/fake/index.ts` is not changed: the runner and several committed results use it.
2. Add a capability-honesty case to the conformance suite: for each flag an adapter sets, the behaviour the flag promises. Add the broken variants the suite's own meta-test needs.
3. Add `revocation.test.ts`: per registered store, write a policy item, supersede it, read through `Plane.read` under direct and indirect insertion, and assert the revoked item is not admitted and does not outrank its replacement.
4. Amend ADR 0013 for Mem0 version 3 (its open-source package no longer has graph memory; UPDATE and DELETE behaviour as the version 3 migration guide states it). Re-read the guide; the amendment is proposed, for the lead.

Absorbs (mode per ADR 0037):

- Memory-Rebirth-Attack (Apache-2.0, idea): the scenario grid of arXiv 2609.08258.
- Zep (arXiv 2501.13956) (paper, idea): the capability vocabulary for temporal graphs.

Files: packages/adapters/test/conformance/ (suite.ts, broken.ts, registry.ts, meta.test.ts, and the new validity double), packages/adapters/test/plane/revocation.test.ts (new), docs/decisions/0013-adapter-contract-and-conformance.md (amendment, proposed), docs/log/

Depends on: none (A4, A1, A2 and A3 are merged)

Decision: none. The ADR 0013 amendment joins the lead's list as proposed.

Acceptance: `make test-one T=packages/adapters/test/plane/revocation.test.ts` and `make test`

Risks: The grid may pass at once, which is the result: the entry says so. A case that fails on a real adapter is a finding, never silenced as adapter-specific.

Out of scope: any change under `packages/adapters/src/` or to the Adapter contract (`packages/core/src/adapter.ts`); a Cognee or Graphiti adapter; anything in another package; schema changes unless the card says so (AGENTS.md rule 5)
