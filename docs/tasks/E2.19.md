# E2.19 The approval queue: held writes routed to named approvers, with expiry

Status: planned, absorb plan 2.

Lane: product. Week: W13. Effort: M. Read AGENTS.md, the plan's entry for this card and `docs/conventions/absorption.md` before starting.

Goal: The review queue as a projection over the log: a request is the `gate` event with decision `ask` that `promote.ts` already appends, keyed by the candidate's id and version; the `approval` block routes requests to named approvers, expires them by a recorded deny at the injected clock, and refuses self-approval.

Why: Govern's trusted organisational memory, and Gate D's Knowledge dogfood (item 28): under the knowledge profile every team and org write is a candidate.

Steps:

1. The projection and its states: pending, approved, denied, expired.
2. Routing: the owner, a role, a list.
3. Expiry as an event; self-approval refused.
4. A new invariant in its own property file: no candidate becomes active without an allow by a routed approver.
