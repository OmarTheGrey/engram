Status: planned, absorb plan 2.

Why: Rules 4, 6 and 7 underpin H1's replay claim and are enforced today by convention. Cards L7 and X14 each met tests that broke only under `ENGRAM_OFFLINE=1`, and card R4 found crosswalk claim C44 (injected time) has no test.

2. Tests that legitimately fetch pinned data declare it by a named helper; list each.
3. `scripts/test/core-determinism-rule.test.ts`, shaped like `pi-import-rule.test.ts`: `Date.now`, argument-free `new Date()`, `performance.now` and `Math.random` are refused in core outside `src/db/postgres*.ts`.
