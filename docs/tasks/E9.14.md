# E9.14 The signed erasure receipt, with residue verification

Status: planned, absorb plan 2.

From `docs/tasks/absorb-plan-2.md`, pull request 26 of 30 (research plans R-085 (GH-48) in `docs/research/absorb/long-list.md`). Lane: product. Week: W14. Effort: M. Read AGENTS.md, the plan's entry for this card and `docs/conventions/absorption.md` before starting.

Goal: `engram erase --receipt` writes an Ed25519-signed, content-free receipt of an offboarding; `engram verify-receipt` rereads the stores and the log and answers unchecked, valid or invalid, naming what it cannot prove.

Why: Twin's erasure as evidence a third party can check; GDPR Article 17 in the crosswalk.

Steps:

1. The receipt's canonical JSON and a Merkle root over the redaction manifest.
2. Signing through `packages/cli/src/signing.ts`; verification with a trusted key set.
3. Tamper tests: an edited receipt, a flipped hash, a swapped key, a truncated tail.
