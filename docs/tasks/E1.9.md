Goal: The pi extension's log opener keeps the chain; each event's attestation digest joins its chain record under a chain version; `migrate` is safe when two processes open a fresh log at once.

Why: An edited attestation verifies clean today; a handle outside the shared opener breaks the chain; two processes can fail with "table events already exists".

Steps:

1. Failing tests: an edited attestation, an event appended by the pi extension's handle, two processes migrating.
2. `keepChain` on the pi extension's opener.
3. A chain version; the attestation digest in the record, in the same transaction; `engram log verify` reads both versions.
4. `migrate` re-reads `schema_migrations` inside each migration's transaction.
