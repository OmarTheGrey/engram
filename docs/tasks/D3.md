Roadmap v1's card was "Replay validation gate: re-run originating tasks with the candidate installed; admit at or above threshold via promote", files `procedures/validate.py`, acceptance "a failing candidate stays candidate; a passing one becomes active". Under ADR 0001 the paths are the TypeScript layout in AGENTS.md.

Goal: A candidate procedure is admitted only after it passes a replay: tasks it was not induced from are rerun with it installed, and it becomes active through `Plane.promote` when it helps on at least k of n.

Why: H5's claim is that a procedure admitted after a replay helps more than one admitted unchecked. The 2026 literature agrees that unchecked skill libraries drift below having none. The admission states and the promotion call exist (card E2.5); the replayer that decides does not.

Steps:

1. A split made by construction: the tasks a candidate was induced from are never among those it is replayed on. A test asserts it for every candidate.
2. The replayer in the runner: for each held-out task, one session without the candidate and one with it, seeded and cached, on the scripted model for the fixture.
3. The oracle is the task's own check: tau2's database-state hash where the task has one (card B11), the fixture's stated outcome otherwise.
4. Admission: at least k of n held-out tasks at or above the threshold promotes the candidate through `Plane.promote`; anything less leaves it a candidate, with the event's reason carrying the counts. k, n and the threshold come from the policy's promotion rule.
5. A replayed fixture where one candidate is admitted and one is refused.

Absorbs (mode per ADR 0037):

- tau2-bench (MIT, idea): its database-state reward as the replay oracle.
- SKILL.nb, Library Drift (papers, idea): what drift looks like, for the fixture's refused candidate.
