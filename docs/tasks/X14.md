# X14 The red-team harness

Status: open.

From `docs/tasks/absorb-plan.md`, pull request 23 of 30 (research plans R-038 in `docs/research/absorb/long-list.md`). Lane: research. Week: W14. Effort: L. Read AGENTS.md, the plan's entry for this card and `docs/conventions/absorption.md` before starting.

Goal: A seeded, replayable attack suite against `Plane.write` and `Plane.read` under the registered conditions, scored against benign controls, so that what the content guards and trust floors stop, and what they wrongly stop, is one table.

Why: Cards E2.9, E2.10 and E2.11 claim to stop things. A claim like that needs an attack suite the cards did not write for themselves, with controls, or it is marketing.

Steps:

1. The corpus: OWASP's 21 AMSB scenarios at commit `b052787cc342` (15 attacks and 6 benign controls, counted again at the commit the card pins) with their severity grades, vendored as data with attribution; the deepset and Lakera payload sets, pinned in `packages/bench/PINS.md`; and benign controls of the same shape.
2. Two routes: the write route, an attack that enters by a write through `Plane.write`, and the read route, one that enters by what a read returns (content placed in the store outside the plane and adopted at read). They are not named after MemForm's Selection and Path, which mean retention and consolidation order (`docs/readings/litreview/2610.14431-memform.md`); an attack on those is a later extension. It extends the poisoning arm of ADR 0029 rather than replacing it.
3. Per scenario: whether the planted content survives to a later read (canary survival), under each condition and with each guard off and on.
4. Scoring with Wilson intervals, attacks and controls side by side, by `research/scripts/redteam.py`.
5. `make redteam` runs offline from cassettes on the scripted model. A live run is a separate decision after the arm is registered.

Absorbs (mode per ADR 0037):

- OWASP Agent Memory Guard (Apache-2.0, copy): the AMSB corpus, as data with attribution.
- deepset prompt-injections (Apache-2.0, depend): payload data, pinned.
- Lakera prompt-injection data (MIT, depend): payload data, pinned.
- garak, PyRIT (Apache-2.0, MIT, idea): probe and orchestration structure.
- mnemosyne (MIT, idea): a seeded, byte-stable poisoning corpus with benign controls.

Files: packages/bench/src/redteam/ (new), packages/bench/src/amsb/ (new), packages/bench/test/redteam/ (new), fixtures/bench/amsb/ (new), packages/bench/PINS.md, research/scripts/redteam.py with its config, result and MANIFEST row (new; the result named under H1's prefix, `research/results/h1-redteam.json`, since `research/tests/test_prereg.py` maps every MANIFEST row to one hypothesis), fixtures/cassettes/x14-redteam-fixture/ (new), Makefile and AGENTS.md with CLAUDE.md, docs/absorbed.md, NOTICE, docs/log/

Depends on: R2 (the arm proposed), E2.9, E2.10; E2.11 if merged

Decision: none in the plan. Registration of the arm is the lead's; a new pin is its own PR under rule 8.

Acceptance: `make redteam` offline from cassettes, and `make test-repro` with the new row

Risks: Twenty-one scenarios, 15 of them attacks, is a small sample and a self-chosen corpus is optimistic: both are stated beside the table. Overlap with MEM-INV-Bench's channels is mapped, not double counted.

Out of scope: a live run; any claim of defence beyond the measured table; anything in another package; schema changes unless the card says so (AGENTS.md rule 5)
