From `docs/tasks/absorb-plan-2.md`, section 9, "Gate work cards (2026-11-25)". Lane: research. Week: W8, merged by 30 November (the day after W8 ends). It merges first, before card X20. Effort: S. Read AGENTS.md, the pre-registration's Statistics section ("Seeds and exclusions") and the header of `research/analysis/h1.py` before starting.

Goal: The H1 table reports, per arm and condition, how many GateMem utility sessions the turn limit capped, beside the exclusion counts it already reports, so the sessions that leave MGS's U are visible where the result is read.

Why: Pre-tag condition 3 says items 14 and 15 are built before the tag, and the Statistics section says this one is not. Today a capped utility session reaches the table as a utility row with `utility_ok: null` (`packages/runner/src/research/h1-sessions.ts`, line 152), the same as a utility session whose judge gave no verdict, and the schema forbids an `exclusion` on a utility row (`research/analysis/h1.py`, line 224). So U loses those sessions silently, which item 15 answered (a) against.

Sources: pre-registration, pre-tag condition 3, Statistics and H1; `docs/tasks/lead-decisions.md`, item 15 and its row (a); backlog row "Capped utility sessions in the H1 table"; the cards' review of 2026-11-26 (findings X20-3, X20-4, X21-1 and X21-2).
