# L5 The near-duplicate lint ADR 0018 promised

Status: open.

From `docs/tasks/absorb-plan.md`, pull request 16 of 30 (research plans R-034 in `docs/research/absorb/long-list.md`). Lane: research. Week: W12. Effort: M. Read AGENTS.md, the plan's entry for this card and `docs/conventions/absorption.md` before starting.

Goal: The duplicate lint finds near-duplicates without embeddings: word-shingle MinHash for candidates and a precision tier that vetoes pairs whose rare terms, numbers or polarity differ. With the threshold unset, every committed result is byte-identical.

Why: ADR 0018 names a MinHash near-duplicate lint and card L1 built only the exact one. On LongMemEval's own memory the exact-duplicate lint is the only deterministic lint that fires (967 findings in `research/results/h3-lint-floor.json`), so a near-duplicate tier is the cheapest way to give H3's deterministic floor more to say.

Steps:

1. MinHash with a fixed seed inside `duplicates()` in `packages/core/src/lint/det.ts`, over word shingles, with character shingles below a length floor. Active only when the policy's near-duplicate threshold is set. The finding carries the estimated Jaccard similarity.
2. The precision tier: IDF-weighted cosine over the scope's items, with a veto when the highest-IDF terms of the two items differ, and a veto on differing numbers and on opposite polarity.
3. A research script checks precision and recall on the fault catalogue against datasketch as an oracle, on the research side only.
4. `make test-repro` shows no committed hash moved with the threshold unset.

Absorbs (mode per ADR 0037):

- claude-mem (Apache-2.0, port): the IDF cosine and IDF veto (`nearDuplicate.ts`, `idf.ts`, `idfVeto.ts`).
- codebase-memory-mcp (MIT, port): the MinHash layout; its parameters are re-derived, not trusted.
- hermes-dreaming (MIT, idea): the veto on differing numbers, names and polarity.
- obsidian-mind (MIT, idea): title-weighted Jaccard as a comparison point.
- datasketch (MIT, idea): the oracle, in research code only.

Files: packages/core/src/lint/det.ts, packages/core/test/unit/lint/, research/scripts/near_duplicate.py with its config and result (new), research/results/MANIFEST.md, docs/absorbed.md, docs/decisions/0018-duplicates-without-embeddings.md (a note that the promise is kept), docs/log/

Depends on: none (L1 and L4 are merged). The threshold key exists: `near-duplicate` in `LINT_THRESHOLD_KEYS`, `packages/core/src/lint.ts`.

Decision: none. Using the tier in H3 needs its arm registered (card R2).

Acceptance: `make test`, and `make test-repro` with every earlier hash unchanged

Risks: ADR 0018 forbids embeddings by default: none are used. A pairwise pass is quadratic; banding keeps it near linear and the test measures it at 10,000 items.

Out of scope: write-time deduplication beyond the exact hash; merging duplicates (the lint reports, a person decides); anything in another package; schema changes unless the card says so (AGENTS.md rule 5)
