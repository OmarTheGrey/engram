proof of concept: the system shown working on fixtures, scripted models or a model's own traces (a zero-leak count, mutants killed, traces replayed); decides no hypothesis.

Pre-registration section “U6. The paired fault-injection arm, for H3 (not scheduled)”.

Card X18's check of proposal U6, which is not registered (the lead registered U7 only): a proof of concept that supports H3 and is not H3's test, measuring state recovery, not H3's utility recovery.
