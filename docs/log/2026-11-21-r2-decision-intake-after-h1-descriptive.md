**What.** The intake note states that a benchmark taken in after 1 December enters only a descriptive arm of its own, never a registered hypothesis's primary set.

**Why.** Under lead-decisions item 9, H2 to H5 are fixed before H1's data exist, and H1 runs from 1 to 8 December. Adding a benchmark to H2 or H3 once H1's numbers are known would make that hypothesis's design depend on data already seen.

**Result.** No intake candidate is proposed for a registered hypothesis. LongMemEval-V2 and the other admissible benchmarks would each need an arm entry.

**Consequence.** If the lead wants LongMemEval-V2 in H2, it has to be registered and pinned before H1 runs, which is a tight window before the tag on 30 November.
