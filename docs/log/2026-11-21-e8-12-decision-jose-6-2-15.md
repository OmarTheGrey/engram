**What.** jose is pinned at 6.2.15, not the 6.2.17 the card names.

**Why.** The absorption convention asks for a version at least two weeks old. `npm view jose time` gives 2026-11-09 for 6.2.17 and 2026-11-08 for 6.2.16, both under two weeks on 2026-11-21; 6.2.15 is from 2026-11-01.

**Result.** The releases between them fix only general JWS and JWE option errors and a negative `maxTokenAge`, which Engram does not pass (the GitHub release notes of v6.2.16 and v6.2.17).

**Consequence.** Moving to 6.2.17 is its own change once it is old enough.
