**What.** pi-ai 1.3.0's request options (`StreamOptions`, `SimpleStreamOptions`) carry temperature and maxTokens but no sampling seed.

**Why.** ADR 0016 and AGENTS.md rule 6 assume every sampled call takes a seed from the experiment config.

**Result.** The seed cannot be passed to providers through pi-ai. It is part of the cache key instead, so each seed is one recorded sample and reruns replay it exactly.

**Consequence.** Seeds give reproducible replays and independent replicates, not reproducible fresh samples; the methods chapter says so, and the optional seeded open-weights arm (roadmap X8) is the only bit-exact path.
