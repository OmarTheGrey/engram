**What.** The absorb and state-of-the-art research of 2026-11-21 is committed under `docs/research/absorb/`: seventeen area studies, four gap studies, a GitHub sweep and the merged long list.

**Consequence.** The thirty-PR plan is selected from this list (`docs/tasks/absorb-plan.md`). The reports are notes, not commitments; claims about our own code that were read and not run are tested first (card E2.7).
