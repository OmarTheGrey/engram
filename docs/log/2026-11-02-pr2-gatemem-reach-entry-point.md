**What.** A TypeScript entry point computes, per GateMem access and forgetting checkpoint, which turns the asker can read under ADR 0030's mapping and which leak targets sit in them; `research/scripts/h1_gatemem_reach.py` calls it and aggregates.

**Why.** Plan decision 11: the reachable-leak share is the evidence for the forgetting decision, and the mapping must not be reimplemented in Python.

**Result.** `reachOf` hands the loaded checkpoint to the bench's `mapCheckpoint` (which strips hidden fields first), writes every turn through `Plane.write` as the C5 probe does, and reads as the asker with `Plane.read`. `scoreReach`, a separate part, reads the hidden labels and uses the scorer's own matcher and context-leak check. A test changes every hidden field of the 14 fixture checkpoints and requires the same reach. New files only; no existing runner or bench source was edited.

**Consequence.** The share is regenerated by `make test-repro`.
