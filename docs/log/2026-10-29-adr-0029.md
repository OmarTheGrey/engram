**What.** The poisoning arm's benchmark was chosen and dated: MEM-INV-Bench, recorded as ADR 0029, pending the lead's review.

**Why.** Gate A exception (b) orders the choice (MPBench if released, else MEM-INV-Bench, else BF1); MPBench has no release as of 2026-10-29, and MEM-INV-Bench's code and data are released under MIT at `cf3c9a4`.

**Result.** Card B4 builds the arm through the plane on a vendored fixture subset; registration waits for the pre-registration to leave the backlog, where the benchmark is already a proposed amendment. The arm measures poisoned memory driving an action past an authorisation monitor, not MPBench's store-then-retrieve design, and H1's poisoning claim is worded accordingly.

**Consequence.** PR 6 of the Gate B plan; MPBench may still be added by a decision before card X3 if it is released.
