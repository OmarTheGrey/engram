**What.** Card E2.5 lands promotion: the pure `evaluatePromotion`, `Plane.promote` and `engram promote <item-id> --to <scope> [--review]`.

**Why.** ADR 0026: confirmations are independent corroboration, reviewers are user-sourced non-owner members, and a promotion is a write under the target's rule.

**Result.** The acceptance command passes, 63 tests: a fixture promotes only at the third distinct actor with a trusted source (the same actor's restatements and model or compaction writes do not count), the review path records the approver and refuses the owner and a model-sourced actor, a target requiring review asks with one gate event, a procedural candidate without a validation record stays candidate, a widening below the team floor is denied under `<policy>/write/team`, and a promotion landing in a full scope is never itself evicted. Each of five mutated rules fails the unit tests.

**Consequence.** Card D3 must supply validation records before any procedure becomes active.
