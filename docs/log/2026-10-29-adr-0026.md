**What.** ADR 0026 (Promotion: confirmations are independent corroboration, reviewers are user-sourced members, a promotion is a write under the target's rule) was drafted as accepted, pending the lead's review, on the lead's instruction.

**Why.** ADR 0011 says "k confirmations or review" without defining either. Origin-Bound Authority proves planted corroboration launders authority, so confirmations must come from different actors with trusted provenance kinds; Authorization Laundering shows self-granted authority is acted on, so a reviewer must be a user-sourced member other than the owner.

**Result.** `docs/decisions/0026-promotion-corroboration-and-review.md`: confirmations counted from the log by distinct actor and trusted kind; reviewer rule; procedural admission needs a validation record; a promotion is evaluated as a write into the target scope; class or scope changes are new versions with lineage, status changes in place.

**Consequence.** PR 5 of the plan builds it and `engram promote`. The lead accepts, amends or reverses it.
