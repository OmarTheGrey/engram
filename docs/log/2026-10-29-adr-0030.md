**What.** GateMem's principals become person-level actors, each turn an episodic item in its speaker's person scope, and unrestricted relationships become team memberships plus read grants on the speakers' turns; recorded as ADR 0030, pending the lead's review.

**Why.** Plan decision 7 replaces RN1's placeholder with a mapping read only from the fields `strip.ts` lets an agent see. Relationships are read by field shape, not by their 90 undocumented type names, so no per-type judgement made beside the labels enters the condition.

**Result.** Changing every hidden field of every fixture checkpoint leaves the mapping identical (fast-check, seed 20261029). Restricted relationships (`access_scope` and similar) map to nothing, since grants cannot narrow by topic.

**Consequence.** Scores under the mapping are about the plane; topic restrictions, deletions stated in dialogue and a turn's group are not expressible, and are reported as limitations.
