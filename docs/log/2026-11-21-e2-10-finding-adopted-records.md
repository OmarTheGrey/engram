**What.** Adoption records a store's item at the least trusted of what it claims, what the plane holds for its id, and `import`, so a record new to the plane that claims the user's word lands as `import`, which the four kind-conditioned rules never read.

**Why.** Review E2.10-4. A store can hold what a tool wrote directly, outside the plane.

**Result.** Stated as a limit of the result; a record that claims tool or model, or that the plane holds as such, keeps that kind and is screened by all eight.

**Consequence.** A backlog row asks the lead whether adopted records should get the kind-conditioned rules.
