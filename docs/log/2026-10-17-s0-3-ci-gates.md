**What.** Step-0 task S0.3 added the convention gates: `check-pr.ts` (the PR body has an `Acceptance:` line with a real command, and the diff adds a valid docs/log entry), a full log validator (field set, enums, real dates, H1 to H5, evidence paths exist, supersedes points at an entry, body sections in order, at most 150 words), fixture PRs under `fixtures/ci/`, and `conventions.yml` running the gate on every pull request.

**Why.** AGENTS.md rule 14 and the documentation convention say CI rejects a PR without a log entry or an acceptance command.

**Result.** `make lint-docs PR=fixtures/ci/pr-no-log` fails with "PR diff must add or change an entry under docs/log/"; `PR=fixtures/ci/pr-valid` passes; both are asserted in `scripts/test/fixtures-ci.test.ts`. All seven existing entries pass.

**Consequence.** Edits to existing entries are not yet detected; immutability is enforced in review only.
