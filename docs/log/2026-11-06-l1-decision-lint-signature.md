**What.** `Plane.lint(scope)` became `lint(actor, at, scope)` returning a LintReport (decision, findings, events), like `retain`; a non-member of the scope gets one deny event (`engram/lint/membership`) and no findings.

**Why.** The user chose the retain shape: every event needs an actor and an injected time (decision D6), and a finding names item ids, so only a member of the scope may ask. A bare finding list could not say that a call was refused.

**Result.** The `/lint` route takes `{actor, scope, at?}` and answers 200 with the report instead of 501; `engram lint` takes the actor flags and needs `--scope` (the old default `org` is gone); the clients in sdk/ and packages/ui/src/api were regenerated by `make api`. The lint runs in the write queue.

**Consequence.** Callers of the old `/lint` shape break; none existed outside tests. The architecture's Interfaces section gives the new signature.
