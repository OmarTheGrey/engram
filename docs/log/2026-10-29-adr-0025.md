**What.** ADR 0025 (Retention semantics) was drafted as accepted, pending the lead's review, on the lead's instruction.

**Why.** ADR 0010 says retention retires and never deletes, but nothing decided when a rule fires, who runs it, how utility is measured or what `manual` does. The literature review adds two constraints: lifecycle is an admissibility axis a read must check (2610.07309), and every memory system fails forget requests through relearning (2610.18052).

**Result.** `docs/decisions/0025-retention-semantics.md`: a pure evaluator; expiry excluded at read and retired by sweep; budgets enforced in the write queue with recency, utility (reads returned) and manual (an `ask` event) orders; `Plane.retain` run by the surfaces as a scope member; retired content re-admitted only by a user-sourced write.

**Consequence.** PR 4 of the plan builds it, with a fifth invariant. The lead accepts, amends or reverses it.
