**What.** ADR 0049, accepted 2026-11-25 under the lead's instruction, amends ADR 0026: the owner accepts in their own person scope only, as user, never raising trust.

**Why.** ADR 0026 rejected owner approval because it authorises content for others (2609.01836); in the owner's own person scope it authorises nothing for anyone else.

**Result.** No conflict found in ADRs 0004, 0006, 0007, 0011, 0034, 0039, 0040, 0042 or 0046: ADR 0040 already exempts the user-sourced owner, and ADR 0039 says a person promotes a held item.

**Consequence.** The decisions index lists 0049 as accepted; ADR 0026 points to it.
