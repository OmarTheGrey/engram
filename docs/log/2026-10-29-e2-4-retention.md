**What.** Retention as ADR 0025 sets it: a pure evaluator, `Plane.retain` in the write queue, a budget check in each landing write's queue slot, ttl exclusion at read, the retired-content gate on one shared content hash, and calls from the pi extension and `engram read`.

**Why.** Card E2.4 needs "budget exceeded evicts per policy; events recorded", and ADR 0010 needed a mechanism that retires without deleting.

**Result.** The acceptance command passes, 36 tests: all three eviction orders, expiry excluded at read before a sweep and then retired, retired content refused to model, compaction and tool sources and admitted to user, document and import. Eleven mutants (each order, membership, budget boundary, queueing, read exclusion and its boundary, the source list both ways, the rule removed) each turn a test red. A pi session fixture shows a write evicting.

**Consequence.** Card E2.5 reuses `inWriteQueue`, `enforceBudget` and `contentHash`.
