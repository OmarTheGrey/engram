**What.** Card D1 adds the recorder, `packages/runner/src/recorder.ts` (`recordSession`, `recordSessionFiles`). It reads session files, parses and scrubs each one in core, and writes the episode through `Plane.write` as an active episodic item. The actor's source is set by the recorder.

**Why.** Rule 2 says stores are reached only through the plane, and Distill needs governed episodes.

**Result.** Each session is reported as stored, denied (with the gate's event), held (field paths only, no write, no event) or invalid. The item's ref is `pi-session:<id>`. Its hash is the file's sha256 and its session entry is the final leaf. Six recorder tests pass.

**Consequence.** Card D2 can read episodes through `Plane.read`. Recording live sessions inside the pi extension is still out of scope.
