## Claim
Forged reasoning traces in memory, written in evasive language and reinforced by self-reference, defeat keyword filters and consensus defences, and a layered detector (SENTINEL) stops them.

## Method
FARMA inserts forged reasoning traces and amplifies them by self-referential reinforcement. SENTINEL's Reasoning Guard scores a candidate memory for forgery from five weighted signals (not named in the abstract). Compared with a keyword filter, A-MemGuard and consensus defences over 50 trials across agents and models.

## Key numbers
- FARMA attack success up to 100 percent at baseline; SENTINEL as low as 0 percent; no false positives on 326 benign traces (abstract).

## What Engram borrows
A procedural-class fixture: a forged rationale written as a distilled procedure. Distill's replay admission is the natural defence, since a forged procedure must pass replay.

## What Engram does differently
Engram's gate does not score text; it checks origin. A forged trace from a model source cannot reach shared scope.

## Risk to our claims
Within person scope, model-sourced content is allowed and served. A forged rationale there is read back. The thesis should say so.

## Open question
Does replay admission catch a forged procedure whose steps succeed but whose rationale misleads?
