## Claim
A temporal knowledge graph with bi-temporal edges (when a fact was true, when the system learned it) and edge invalidation instead of deletion gives better recall than full context on LongMemEval at a tenth of the latency, and ties MemGPT on DMR.

## Method
Graphiti builds three subgraphs: episodes (raw messages with reference time and actor), entities with fact edges, and communities with summaries (Section 2). Entities are extracted from the current message plus the last four, resolved by embedding and full-text search, and deduplicated by an LLM (2.2.1). Each edge carries t_valid and t_invalid on the world timeline and t_created and t_expired on the system timeline; a contradicting new edge sets the old edge's t_invalid and the old fact is kept (2.2.3). Retrieval combines cosine, BM25 and breadth-first search, reranks with RRF, MMR, episode mentions, node distance or a cross-encoder, and formats facts with date ranges (Section 3). Experiments: DMR with gpt-4-turbo and gpt-4o-mini against MemGPT and full conversation; LongMemEval_s (about 115k tokens per conversation) with gpt-4o-mini and gpt-4o against full context, graded by GPT-4o (Section 4).

## Key numbers
- Table 1 DMR, gpt-4-turbo: Zep 94.8 percent, full conversation 94.4, MemGPT 93.4 (MemGPT's own figure). With gpt-4o-mini: Zep 98.2, full 98.0.
- Table 2 LongMemEval: gpt-4o-mini full-context 55.4 percent at 31.3 s and 115k tokens versus Zep 63.8 at 3.20 s and 1.6k tokens; gpt-4o full-context 60.2 versus Zep 71.2.
- Table 3 by type, gpt-4o: single-session-preference 20.0 to 56.7; temporal reasoning 45.1 to 62.4; knowledge update 78.2 to 83.3; single-session-assistant 94.6 down to 80.4.
- Section 4.2: DMR conversations are about 60 messages each.

## What Engram borrows
- The four timestamps are the model for ADR 0005: `valid_from` and `valid_to` on the item, ingestion time on the event, and an update that sets `valid_to` on the old version rather than overwriting. The expired-validity lint is a comparison on t_valid and t_invalid.
- Invalidate-and-keep is store-native validity; the Zep adapter sets `native_validity` and `native_history` (ADR 0013) so the plane does not duplicate what Graphiti does.
- Formatting facts with "Date range: from - to" (3.2) is a precedent for carrying the validity window in the explanation and into the model's view (ADR 0009).
- Section 4.2's critique of DMR (single-turn, about 60 messages, fits in context) is why DMR is not in the evaluation protocol (ADR 0015).

## What Engram does differently
Zep's edge invalidation is decided by an LLM at ingestion with no reason recorded and no scope: every actor's facts land in one graph. Engram records the supersession as an event with the new evidence's provenance and keeps contradiction detection as a reviewable lint rather than an irreversible ingestion step. Zep's temporal model is per fact; Engram's is per item of any class, including procedures.

## Risk to our claims
Single-session-assistant accuracy drops under Zep on both models (Table 3), and less capable models struggle with the temporal data (4.3.2). A governed read that returns fewer, dated items could show the same loss; H4 may hold for decisions while scores diverge by model. We report per-type LongMemEval results per model, not only the overall number.

## Open question
Zep could not run MemGPT on LongMemEval because it lacked direct message ingestion (4.3.1). Our adapter `write` takes an item, not a conversation; does the Letta adapter need a conversation-ingest path, and is that path governed?
