## Claim
Transforming untrusted input to carry a consistent signal of its origin lets a model separate it from instructions.

## Method
A family of input transformations. The abstract does not describe them; delimiting, datamarking and encoding as the three variants is from memory of the paper and not verified here.

## Key numbers
- Attack success reduced from over 50 percent to below 2 percent, GPT-family models, minimal task impact (abstract).

## What Engram borrows
On read, a returned item whose provenance kind is model, compaction, external or tool is wrapped by the MCP and pi surfaces with a datamark and an origin tag before the model sees it. Whether to mark is a deterministic decision in core's read output (an origin marker on each explained item); the string form belongs in the surfaces. It is a presentation defence, and 2610.11188 finds presentation matters little beside admission.

## What Engram does differently
Spotlighting is a prompt-time defence with no state; Engram's provenance is stored.

## Risk to our claims
Spotlighting needs a model that obeys it. Treat it as defence in depth.

## Open question
Does datamarking change recall utility on LongMemEval? A cheap cassette test.
