## Claim
End-to-end QA accuracy hides where a memory system fails; per-query defect codes for encoding, retrieval and generation show retrieval fails most often.

## Method
Three Examiners (encoding, retrieval, generation) assign multi-label defects on LoCoMo, LongMemEval-S and DynaMem-Bench across seven systems, with a recall-first agentic RAG adaptation and a MemWiki auxiliary structure.

## Key numbers
- LoCoMo evidence recall 70.2% to 95.6% with recall-first agentic RAG.
- Default LoCoMo defects: retrieval 22.1%, encoding 7.7%, generation 6.5%.
- MemWiki: +2.5 points LoCoMo, +2.3 LongMemEval-S.

## What Engram borrows
Defect codes as a diagnostic view in the results dashboard, laid beside Engram's write, read and explain events.

## What Engram does differently
Engram attributes a failure to a policy decision (an event with a reason), not to a model-judged examiner.

## Risk to our claims
If retrieval dominates failures, C5's accuracy gain may be small. Report violation rate beside accuracy, as H1 does.

## Open question
Can an event's reason serve as the encoding-defect label without a model?
