## Claim
An authorization language can be both fast and analyzable if it is kept deliberately small: no loops, no user functions, optional typing. Then a symbolic compiler to SMT is decidable, and questions such as "does this refactor change who is allowed" become solver queries.

## Method
Cedar is implemented in Rust, modelled in Lean, and checked by differential random testing against the Lean model. A symbolic compiler turns policy sets into SMT-LIB formulas; the abstract states the encoding is decidable, sound and complete. The authors compare the authorizer with OpenFGA and Rego on generated inputs. Only the abstract and the SymCC and Lean CLI READMEs were read, not the full paper.

## Key numbers
- The abstract says only that Cedar "performs far better" than OpenFGA and Rego. A search summary gave 28.7x to 35.2x (OpenFGA) and 42.8x to 80.8x (Rego); not confirmed in the abstract, verify in the paper body before citing.
- SymCC README: six verifiable properties (never errors, always allows, always denies, implies, equivalent, disjoint), each with a counterexample variant returning a synthesized request and entity store.
- The abstract page gives no proof counts or solver times.

## What Engram borrows
The idea that policy analysis is a first-class feature: an export of Engram's typed policy to Cedar, so that "strict is never more permissive than medium" and "model-sourced content never lands outside person scope" are solver-checked, not only property-tested. Lands as AB-POL-1 and AB-POL-2, a thesis section on mechanised invariants beside the seven property-tested ones.

## What Engram does differently
Engram keeps a typed evaluator in core (ADR 0006) because it needs per-item expiring grants, a clock, and decisions shaped as MemoryEvents with reasons. Cedar would be a derived, analysis-only artefact, not the decision path.

## Risk to our claims
A Cedar export proves properties of the export. If the export and the evaluator diverge, the proof says nothing about the system. Mitigation: differential tests of the export against the evaluator over generated requests, run in process through cedar-wasm.

## Open question
Which of the seven invariants are expressible without quantifying over an item's grant list? Cedar has no quantifiers, so grants need a modelling trick.
