## Claim
Memory-poisoning defences should be reported with their benign-case cost, and when measured with the model held fixed, four common defences show no detectable accuracy cost on LoCoMo, with small token overhead.

## Method
The author varies only the defence (sanitisation, provenance checking, LLM anomaly detection, a read-time reranker, and all four stacked) holding model, backend, retrieval, prompt and judge fixed. Half of LoCoMo (5 conversations, 750 turns, 255 questions per run, 3 runs per condition) with no attack present. A blind audit checks sampled reranker drops.

## Key numbers
- Sanitisation and provenance checking never triggered; anomaly detection blocked 9 of 2,250 turns (0.4 percent); the reranker dropped 2.2 percent of memories it judged and withheld at least one memory from 14.9 percent of questions.
- Blind audit found content-based justification for 2 of 50 sampled reranker drops.
- Reranker accuracy change plus 0.6 points (95 percent interval minus 2.6 to plus 3.8); intervals of plus or minus 2.4 to 4.6 points overall.
- Token overhead 0 percent (sanitisation, provenance), 1.5 (anomaly), 3.0 (reranker), 4.5 (all four).
- An earlier version's 4.4 point cost was 2.9 points of model swap (v2 to v3 revision).

## What Engram borrows
The design is H2's: vary only the defence, hold everything else fixed, report benign cost with intervals and token overhead beside attack success. Its correction of an earlier version (model mismatch) is a template for a failure log entry. The token-overhead column is the cost ledger H2 already requires.

## What Engram does differently
Their defences are content-based or LLM-based and untested under attack in this paper; Engram's gate is deterministic and is measured on both violation and utility.

## Risk to our claims
A benign-only study with CIs spanning a few points means a null tax is the default expectation for provenance-style defences on LoCoMo. If Engram's tax on conversational data is small, H2's "knee" may be flat. Action: state H2's knee on the benchmark where policies actually bite (GateMem), and report LoCoMo as the near-zero baseline.

## Open question
Is Engram's provenance check also near-never-triggered on benign LoCoMo traffic, and does that count as a tax of zero?
