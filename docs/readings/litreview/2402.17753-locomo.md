## Claim
Machine-generated, human-edited conversations of several hundred turns over up to 32 sessions expose a 36-point gap between the best long-context model and humans on question answering, with temporal and multi-hop questions hardest.

## Method
Two LLM agents with personas and temporal event graphs (up to 25 events, 6 to 12 months) generate dialogues in the generative-agents style, including image sharing; annotators edited about 15 percent of turns and replaced about 19 percent of images (Sec. 3). The ACL version releases 10 conversations averaging 588.2 turns, 27.2 sessions and 16,618 tokens (Table 1, Table 5). Tasks: QA in five categories, event summarization scored with ROUGE and FactScore, multimodal dialogue generation (Sec. 4). QA is scored by token-level F1 after normalisation, with recall@k for RAG variants; no LLM judge (Sec. 4.1). Models: Mistral-7B, Llama-2-70b, Llama-3-70B, gpt-3.5-turbo at 4K to 16K, gemini-1.0-pro, claude-3-sonnet, gpt-4-turbo, and DRAGON-retriever RAG over dialog, observations or summaries (Tables 2, 3).

## Key numbers
- 1,986 questions: single-hop 841, multi-hop 282, temporal 321, open-domain 96, adversarial 446 (Table 5, Appendix B.1, ACL version).
- Overall F1: human 87.9; gpt-4-turbo 51.6; claude-3-sonnet 42.8; gemini-1.0-pro 39.1; gpt-3.5-turbo 23.9 at 4K to 35.9 at 16K; Llama-3-70B 30.1 (Table 2).
- RAG with gpt-3.5-turbo: best 40.6 with observations top-5; dialog top-50 40.5; summary top-10 32.0 (Table 3).
- Event summarization: gpt-4-turbo ROUGE-L 21.6, FactScore F1 48.9 (Table 4).
- The arXiv v1 reports 50 conversations, 304.9 turns, 9,209 tokens; the ACL version and repository have 10 conversations.

## What Engram borrows
LoCoMo is not one of the six primary benchmarks, but every store vendor reports on it, so Engram reuses it in one place: the Mem0 and Letta adapters are smoke-tested against vendor LoCoMo numbers so a conformance failure (ADR 0013) shows before any thesis run. The category split is reused as a stratification key in the label audit. The adversarial category (unanswerable questions) is a ready-made abstention fixture for the unsupported-claim lint (ADR 0008).

## What Engram does differently
LoCoMo scores F1 against short gold answers; Engram's primary benchmarks use deterministic state checks or a vendored judge, and the thesis explains why F1 fits governed recall badly (a correct refusal under policy scores zero). Engram uses no LoCoMo result as evidence for any hypothesis.

## Risk to our claims
Ground Truth First cites the Penfield Labs 2026 audit: 6.4 percent of the answer key (99 of 1,540 questions) is score-corrupting, and an LLM judge accepted 62.81 percent of deliberately wrong answers. An adapter smoke test against vendor numbers inherits that noise, and vendors typically score with an LLM judge rather than F1. Mitigation: the smoke test checks only that the adapter lands within a wide band of the vendor figure, and the thesis says LoCoMo is excluded from hypothesis tests.

## Open question
The ACL version has 1,986 questions; the audit and the Mem0 paper use 1,540. Is that the non-adversarial subset (1,986 minus 446), and does Engram's smoke test use the same subset the vendor used?
