## Claim
Three parallel stores (an episodic timeline, an entity-event graph, a curated fact store), every item carrying origin, timestamp and an evidence pointer, plus a reader that may cite only what it opened and otherwise abstains, top LongMemEval and LoCoMo. Accuracy barely moves across eight backbones while cost moves about 30x, so the memory design, not the model, sets quality.

## Method
Ingestion is a four-stage background pipeline: classify sources (chats, files, agent sessions, connectors), index with embeddings and a lexical index, extract events and documentary entries with an LLM, connect into the graph (Section 3.2). Every item has origin in {scan, infer, doc, dialogue, manual}, an immutable timestamp and an evidence pointer resolving to raw artifacts (Definition 1, 3.1). Retrieval: an intent gate, a source router, three concurrent tool-using searches over dense and lexical indexes with time, tag, source and speaker filters, then a merge with one aggregated confidence (3.3, Algorithm 1). Citation lock: every claim must be supported by an opened item or the answer is abstention (Definition 2). Evaluated on LongMemEval (500 questions) and LoCoMo (1,540), single run, official LLM judge, competitor numbers from their best public reports (4.2, 4.4). Storage is PostgreSQL with pgvector.

## What Engram borrows
- The origin enum {scan, infer, doc, dialogue, manual} is a direct comparison for `provenance.kind` in ADR 0004; "infer" is Engram's `kind: model`.
- The citation lock is a read-side rule Engram can enforce without a model: the explanation lists the items returned, and policy can require that an agent's write cites one of them (unsupported-claim lint, ADR 0008).
- The backbone table with accuracy, median latency and list-price cost is the format ADR 0016 and 0017 require; the cost ledger must record cached and uncached tokens separately.
- Abstention is what a governed read returns when policy filters everything: an explicit empty with a reason.

## What Engram does differently
Agent Zero's provenance is a property of its own store and its evaluation is single-run against competitors' self-reported numbers (4.2, 4.4). Engram applies provenance rules across stores it does not own, runs its own baselines under one pinned judge with three seeds, and adds scope, grants and post-write lints, none of which Agent Zero has.

## Risk to our claims
The 3.4 point backbone span on LongMemEval supports H4 in spirit, but the 12.5 point span on LoCoMo (Table 6) argues against reading "model-agnostic" as "score-invariant." H4 must be stated as invariance of the plane's decisions and explanations, with scores reported per model. The claim that every restricted retrieval variant beats the strongest baseline (5.4) is contradicted by Table 3 (Mastra 94.87 above 94.00): let generated tables, not prose, carry our claims.

## Open question
With benchmarks "near-saturated" (5.1) at 93 to 96 percent, headroom for a visible governance tax is small. Should H2 be measured on a benchmark where ungoverned recall is already below 80 percent?
