## Claim
Poisoning can enter at five memory-formation interfaces: Content, Evidence, Scope, Selection and Path. Defences mitigate incompletely and unevenly.

## Method
A diagnostic benchmark with clean and poisoned conditions per interface, six LLMs, two response formats. Selection isolates retention effects; Path isolates consolidation-order effects.

## Key numbers
None stated in the sources read.

## What Engram borrows
A taxonomy for the red-team harness: Content (the text), Evidence (provenance), Scope (target scope), Selection (what is retained), Path (consolidation order). Evidence and Scope are the gate's own ground.

## What Engram does differently
The gate binds Evidence and Scope deterministically. Selection and Path correspond to retention and promotion order, which no current fixture attacks.

## Risk to our claims
Path poisoning is an attack on distillation and promotion order that no fixture covers.

## Open question
Does promotion order change the outcome under the corroboration rule (ADR 0026)?
