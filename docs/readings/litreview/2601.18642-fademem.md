## Claim
A two-layer memory with differential exponential decay, driven by relevance, access frequency and age, plus LLM conflict resolution and merging, cuts storage 45 percent and helps multi-hop retrieval.

## Method
Evaluated on Multi-Session Chat, LoCoMo and LTI-Bench. A search snippet says an LLM labels a new memory against old ones as compatible, contradictory, subsumes or subsumed. That was not verified in the paper. Abstract only.

## Key numbers
- 45 percent storage reduction (abstract).

## What Engram borrows
The four-way relation label as the output schema of the model contradiction lint (today a two-way verdict), and a decay score as an explainable retention component (ADR 0025).

## What Engram does differently
Decay only orders eviction candidates. Eviction retires and never deletes (ADR 0010).

## Risk to our claims
Decay can drop rare but critical items. The retention-budget invariant must hold it.

## Open question
Does decay-ordered eviction beat the current order on the long-horizon arm?
