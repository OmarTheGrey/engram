## Claim
A layered guardrail of a jailbreak classifier (PromptGuard 2), a reasoning auditor (AlignmentCheck) and a code static analyser (CodeShield) can sit as a last defence in an agent, with custom scanners written as a regex or an LLM prompt.

## Method
Three components. Abstract read only.

## Key numbers
None stated in the sources read.

## What Engram borrows
The scanner interface: a scanner takes a message and returns a score and decision, with regex scanners beside model scanners. Engram's injected injection lint should have that shape, with the classifier supplied at the boundary.

## What Engram does differently
Engram applies the check at the memory write, with origin and scope in hand, and logs it as an event.

## Risk to our claims
Prompt Guard 2 weights (22M and 86M) carry `license:other` on Hugging Face, the Llama community terms; not absorbable under our rule. ProtectAI's `deberta-v3-base-prompt-injection-v2` is Apache-2.0 with an ONNX tag (397,882 downloads, last modified 2026-07-09) and runs through transformers.js (see repo-transformers-js).

## Open question
