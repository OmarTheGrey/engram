## Claim
Generate the facts first and render the conversation from them, and gold answers are valid by construction; under that instrument, memory rankings reverse with history length, because a budgeted curated memory wins at three weeks and loses at nine when eviction removes old material, while a provenance-typed graph improves.

## Method
A seeded life-script sampler emits dated facts with a validity interval, a volatility class and a channel (chat, or email with sent and received distinguished) (Sec. 3.1). Amazon Nova Pro renders text; a verifier checks every planted fact appears; questions are generated from the script (Sec. 3.2 to 3.3). Short-horizon corpus: 14 users, 275 questions; tenure corpus: 6 users, nine-week histories, 108 questions asked as of weeks 3, 6, 9; 383 questions over 15 types (Sec. 3.3). Backends: curated budgeted map, vector (Titan-v2), provenance-typed graph, hybrid v1, hybrid v2 layered, and no memory (Sec. 4). Answerer claude-haiku-4-5, primary judge claude-sonnet-5, cross-family judge deepseek.v3.2, three replicates (Sec. 3.4).

## Key numbers
- Tenure accuracy, weeks 3 / 6 / 9: map 81.2 / 79.6 / 78.4; vector 75.3 / 68.8 / 74.7; graph 75.9 / 79.0 / 90.4; hybrid v2 80.2 / 85.8 / 93.2; none 16.0 / 16.4 / 16.7 (Table 3, Sec. 5.4).
- Early-chapter probe: map 96.3 to 72.2 percent; graph 94.4 to 100; vector 88.9 to 70.4 (Sec. 5.4, Figure 2).
- Crossover interaction (graph minus map, week 9 minus week 3): +17.3 pp, five of six users, p 0.063 under the primary judge; +24.1 pp, all six, p 0.031 cross-family (Sec. 5.4).
- Full history holds the probe at 160 of 162; no judge-independent winner against hybrid v2 at week 9, at roughly twice the read cost (Sec. 5.4, abstract).
- Short horizon: hybrid v2 96.8, map 94.2, graph 93.2, vector 91.0, none 15.2 (Table 2).
- Write-stage quality: miss rate 24.2 percent for weak writes vs 1.6 for clean, n 1,092, odds ratio 19.6, CI [10.6, 50.3] (Sec. 5.5).
- Injection: the graph had zero unsupported assertions over 42 trials; hybrid v2's wiki 40 of 42 (Sec. 5.7).
- LoCoMo: the Penfield Labs (2026) audit found 6.4 percent of the answer key score-corrupting, 99 of 1,540 questions, and an LLM judge accepting 62.81 percent of deliberately wrong answers (Sec. 1, 2.2, 3.1).

## What Engram borrows
The tenure crossover is why ADR 0010 says retention never deletes and eviction retires, and why the long-horizon arm exists. The write-stage finding justifies ADR 0008 running lints at write time, with missing provenance as the first deterministic check. Volatility class becomes an optional item field used by the expired-validity lint (ADR 0005). The Penfield number is why ADR 0015 mandates a 50-item label audit per benchmark. Veracium's `EvidenceAuthor` and `EvidenceContext` map onto ADR 0004's provenance object.

## What Engram does differently
This paper compares architectures, each owning its write path. Engram puts one policy layer above several stores and measures the layer. Its corpus is fully synthetic; Engram also uses benchmarks with real-looking dialogue, accepting label noise for external validity.

## Risk to our claims
Rankings depend on horizon. If a governed store beats an ungoverned one only because retirement keeps items replayable, and the ungoverned store catches up when its budget rises, the H1 effect is a retention effect, not enforcement. Mitigation: the ungoverned condition gets the same retention budget, and the long-horizon arm reports both.

## Open question
Six tenure users, p 0.031 at best. Should Engram regenerate the corpus with more seeds through Veracium's generator before using the crossover as a planning assumption?
