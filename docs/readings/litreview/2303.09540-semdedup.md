## Claim
Pairs that are semantically alike but not identical can be found with pretrained embeddings and removed with little loss, so semantic duplicates are a class beyond exact and shingle-overlap duplicates.

## Method
Embed items, cluster with k-means, compute pairwise cosine similarity inside clusters, drop one of each pair above a threshold. Evaluated on LAION and C4 for training efficiency. The clustering recipe is from a search summary of NVIDIA's NeMo Curator page, not the paper text.

## Key numbers
- Removes 50% of a LAION subset with minimal loss, about halving training time (abstract).
- On C4, outperforms prior dedup methods (abstract); the 15% efficiency figure is from a search summary only.

## What Engram borrows
Cluster, then compare within a scope, as the shape of a semantic near-duplicate lint: advisory, reporting cosine and the threshold. Lands in AB-RET-4.

## What Engram does differently
Engram flags and never deletes (ADR 0010), compares only within one scope and class, and records the embedding model id and revision in the finding.

## Risk to our claims
Cosine thresholds transfer poorly between embedding models, so a lint tuned on one may over-flag on another.

## Open question
What threshold separates restatement from contradiction on the L2 fault set, where a contradiction can score as close as a paraphrase?

## Notes
