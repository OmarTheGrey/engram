## Claim
One alignment function trained on 4.7M examples from seven tasks (NLI, QA, paraphrase, fact verification, retrieval, similarity, summarisation) scores factual consistency better than task-specific metrics, with 355M parameters.

## Method
A single scorer evaluated on 22 datasets, 19 of them unseen in training. Only the abstract was read.

## Key numbers
- 4.7M training examples, 7 source tasks, 22 evaluation datasets (19 unseen), 355M parameters (abstract).

## What Engram borrows
A continuous support score for the unsupported-claim lint, so a threshold can live in policy (LINT_THRESHOLD_KEYS) instead of a binary verdict.

## What Engram does differently
MiniCheck (2404.10774) is the newer comparison point; AlignScore is a research-side baseline, not the product pick.

## Risk to our claims
Unmaintained since March 2024 and Python-only. No ONNX export was verified.

## Open question
Is a threshold tuned on long grounding documents stable on short memory statements?
