## Claim
Information-flow control gives an agent deterministic security guarantees: a planner tracks confidentiality and integrity labels and enforces policy on them, and the paper classifies which properties dynamic taint tracking can enforce.

## Method
FIDES is a planner with confidentiality and integrity labels, deterministic policy enforcement, and primitives to hide information selectively. Evaluated on AgentDojo. Only a search summary was read.

## Key numbers
None stated in the sources read, beyond the claim that it broadens the tasks that can be done securely on AgentDojo.

## What Engram borrows
Two-axis labels. Provenance is Engram's integrity label; scope and grants are its confidentiality label. ADR 0004 already says a derived item inherits the least trusted provenance of its inputs; FIDES suggests the same join for confidentiality (the most restrictive scope), which matters for Twin person and role scopes.

## What Engram does differently
Labels live on stored items and events, not on values in a running planner.

## Risk to our claims
A reviewer may call Engram's provenance a coarse integrity label. The answer is the lineage rule and the join.

## Open question
Does a derived item (distillation, compaction) take the most restrictive scope of its inputs today, or only the least trusted source?
