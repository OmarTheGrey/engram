## Claim
A half-life recency prior blended with similarity surfaces the newest relevant item where cosine alone fails, though the result is partial and parameter-sensitive.

## Method
A model-agnostic temporal layer for RAG on cybersecurity data (NVD CVEs): a fused semantic-temporal score with half-life decay, plus a heuristic topic-evolution tracker. Abstract only; the half-life value and the formula were not seen.

## Key numbers
- Latest@10 of 0.60 against 0.20 for semantic-then-newest on a hard NVD test; cosine-only 0.00 (abstract).
- Trend tracker macro-F1 0.08, rising to 0.96 after label and noise fixes (abstract).

## What Engram borrows
Supports the shape of ADR 0009's recency component (half-life decay, 30 days today) and making the half-life a policy field per class, logged with the read. Lands in AB-RET-1.

## What Engram does differently
Engram reports recency as its own component and never lets it override validity or scope.

## Risk to our claims
Recency can bury old facts that remain valid, so one global half-life is wrong.

## Open question
Which classes (preference, fact, procedure) deserve a longer half-life?
