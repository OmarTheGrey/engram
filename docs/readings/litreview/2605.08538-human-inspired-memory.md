## Claim
Dedup-based sleep-phase consolidation, interference-based forgetting and reconsolidation on retrieval shrink a store sharply with little or no recall loss at a fixed context budget.

## Method
Six mechanisms and a synthetic threshold calibration, evaluated on a VSCode issue dataset and LongMemEval S and M tiers, with a nine-configuration ablation. Abstract only.

## Key numbers
- VSCode issues (13K issues, 120K events): 97.2 percent retention precision, 58 percent store reduction (abstract).
- LongMemEval M tier: 70.1 percent against 71.2 percent for raw retrieval, overlapping 95 percent CIs (abstract).

## What Engram borrows
Dedup-first consolidation as the safe core of a consolidation job: merge only exact and near duplicates and keep the sources.

## What Engram does differently
Consolidation is a governed write with lineage, never an in-place rewrite (ADR 0005, 0010).

## Risk to our claims
No code, so nothing reproduces. The numbers are the authors' own.

## Open question
How much of the gain survives when sources are retained and only retired?
