## Claim
Agent-memory systems that soft-revoke a fact (mark it invalid and keep it) do not enforce the mark at retrieval, so a revoked policy is returned above its replacement and agents act on it. Enforcement has to sit between the agent and the backend.

## Method
Five systems (Graphiti and Zep CE, mem0 with and without its read-time expiry filter, langmem, cognee) are loaded with a revoked policy and its replacement over nine scenarios and nine models. Six defence conditions are scored: none, store-level filter, prompt hardening, output filter, filter plus prompt hardening, and a stale-retrieval guard that sits between the agent and any backend.

## Key numbers
- Graphiti and mem0 (exp.) return the revoked record in all 81 scenario-model cells, ranked above the replacement (Table 6).
- Unsafe action 43.1 percent pooled (699 of 1,620 trials) undefended.
- Store-level filter 0 percent; prompt hardening 37.2 percent; output filter 18.1 percent (Table 6).
- Even with an explicit prohibition in the prompt, the unsafe action appears in 12.9 percent of trials.
- Toggling one mem0 retrieval flag changed 39 matched cells and never the reverse (Table 13).
- The guard caught only 4 of 14 revoked Zep edges, because one policy was split across edges.

## What Engram borrows
Direct support for H1's claim that instruction is weaker than enforcement on a second measure besides GateMem: filtering at retrieval gives 0, prompt hardening 37.2. The nine-scenario grid is a fixture shape for an adapter conformance test: write, revoke, read, and expect the revoked item never admitted, for every adapter. That revocation marks are invisible under indirect insertion argues for a plane-owned validity field rather than store-native flags.

## What Engram does differently
Their guard infers conflicts lexically; Engram reads supersession from keyed events, so it is exact on keyed items and silent on unkeyed ones.

## Risk to our claims
The guard's weakness on split policies is the unkeyed-item wall again (see 2606.26511). H3 must keep keyed and unkeyed items apart.

## Open question
Which of Engram's three adapters (files, Mem0, Letta) would fail the nine-scenario grid if the plane's filter were off?
