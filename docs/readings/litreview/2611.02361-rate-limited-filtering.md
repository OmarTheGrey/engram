## Claim
A model that must read a growing log to decide whether an action is currently allowed degrades with log length, while an explicitly maintained current state does not, and the crossover depends on the reader model.

## Method
An executable access-log benchmark with a known policy asks whether a service may act now. Open 7B to 32B models read the full history; reasoning-trained models read the current state only; a parse-then-rule filter has a model parse each line and an explicit rule apply updates. Memory is framed as recursive filtering over a state whose size depends on the number of tracked keys. A reader model predicts accuracy in a registered test.

## Key numbers
- Open 7B to 32B models: 72 to 86 percent at 8 to 16 events, 48 to 55 percent at 256 events (abstract).
- Reasoning-trained 32B models with explicit current state: 96 and 98 percent (abstract).
- A 120B reasoning model reading the log trails the state approach by about 4 points at 256 events, 7 at 512 and about 10 at 1,024 (abstract).
- Parse-then-rule filter: 80 to 89 percent at 256 events; models applying parsed updates themselves reach 58 to 59 percent (abstract).
- Reader model predicts accuracy within 0.02 on average (abstract).

## What Engram borrows
Supports the long-context versus memory ablation in H2 with a mechanism: the plane's log is the full history and its projected state (current grants, validity, supersession) is the compact state, so the right test is "log in context" against "projected state in context" for permission questions. Their parse-then-rule split mirrors our separation of model extraction from deterministic policy.

## What Engram does differently
Their task is a synthetic access log; Engram applies the idea to memory items with scope and validity. They do not govern writes.

## Risk to our claims
Gap closes for a strong reader (a 120B reasoning model trails by only about 4 points at 256 events), so the argument for explicit state is weaker as models improve. Action: do not claim long context cannot do this; claim cost and auditability.

## Open question
At what log length does a frontier model on M1 stop matching the projected state for a grant question?
